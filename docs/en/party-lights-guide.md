# Party lights that make your party ten times better

There is a reason clubs and concerts spend serious money on lighting: **light changes the whole mood**. The good news is that you do not need a professional light show to make your party markedly better. A couple of well-placed LED lights and a fog machine make an enormous difference.

## Why does light matter so much?

Think of the difference between the ceiling light on, and the ceiling light off with a few coloured spots in the corners. Those are two completely different evenings. Light means that:

- **The dancefloor becomes inviting** — people step onto it far more willingly when the lighting is dimmed and colourful
- **The mood changes** — from a pleasant gathering to a party
- **Photos and video look better** — your guests will want to share the evening

## What is in our light package?

Our party light package is put together to be easy to set up and to give the most effect:

- **LED par lights** — colour-changing LED spots that stand on the floor or mount on stands. They cover your dancefloor in coloured light.
- **A fog machine** — fog catches the beams and creates the classic club feeling. Without fog, light effects are far less visible.
- **All cables and remote controls**

## Placement tips

1. **The ceiling light has to go off.** This is the most important rule. A bare ceiling light kills any party mood. Turn it off and let the LED spots and a few candles do the rest.

2. **Put the LED spots in the corners.** Two spots in opposite corners of your dance area gives good coverage. Aim them towards the middle.

3. **The fog machine behind the speaker.** Place it behind or beside your sound system so the fog drifts out across the dancefloor. Use it in short bursts — too much fog is also too much.

4. **Combine it with sound.** Light and sound belong together. When the bass lands and the light dances in the fog, you have a party. Combine the light package with a [speaker package](/en#book) for the full effect.

## When are party lights worth it?

- **A birthday in a flat** — ceiling off, LED spots on, music up
- **A garden party after sunset** — outdoor LED spots make a garden look magical
- **A company party or team event** — lifts the evening from obligatory to actually fun
- **A confirmation party** — the younger guests will love a miniature nightclub
