# Sound for a garden party — a guide to getting outdoor audio right

Garden parties are wonderful. But outdoor sound is a different discipline from indoors. It disperses in every direction, the wind carries it away, and the neighbours hear all of it. Here are concrete tips for getting the best sound at your garden party.

## Why is outdoor sound harder?

Indoors, the walls, ceiling and floor reflect sound back to you. Outdoors you get none of those reflections. The result is that you need more output to reach the same perceived volume.

A Bluetooth speaker that fills a living room perfectly disappears completely in a garden. You need a real system.

## Five tips for good outdoor sound

### 1. Put the speaker up high

Stand it on a stand so the sound travels over head height. Sound that reaches your guests' ears directly is far more effective than sound fired along the ground.

### 2. Point it at the guests

Aim the speaker straight at the area where people actually are. Avoid pointing it at a fence or an open space where the sound simply leaves.

### 3. Place it behind the "stage"

If you have a dance area or a central gathering point, put the speaker behind it — so the music comes from behind and fills the space.

### 4. Do not forget the subwoofer

Bass disappears quickly outdoors. A subwoofer makes an enormous difference to the overall experience. It can stand on the ground — bass is not directional the way mid-range is.

### 5. Think about the neighbours

This one matters. Good rules:

- **Keep the music low before 6 PM** and bring it down again **after 10 PM**
- **Tell the neighbours in advance** — a friendly text or a note in the stairwell works wonders
- **Point the speaker away from them** — direction makes a surprising difference
- **Go easy on the bass late in the evening** — bass carries further than mid-range
