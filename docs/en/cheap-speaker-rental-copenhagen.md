## Renting versus buying — which pays off?

Let us be honest: a decent powered speaker costs from 2.000 to 5.000 DKK to buy. A set with two tops, a subwoofer and stands quickly runs to 10.000-15.000 DKK.

And then you have to store it all. In a Copenhagen flat, space is a luxury.

**When renting makes sense:**

- You throw a party 1-4 times a year
- You have nowhere to store bulky equipment
- You want professional audio without the investment
- You would rather not think about maintenance

**When buying makes sense:**

- You use it every week (DJ, musician, event planner)
- You have the storage space

For most private customers, renting is the smarter choice. You get professional equipment for a fraction of the price.
