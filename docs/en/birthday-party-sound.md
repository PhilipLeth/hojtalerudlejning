# Sound for a birthday party — the complete guide

A birthday without music is just... a meeting. Whether you are turning 30, hosting a teenager's party, or arranging a children's birthday, good sound makes the whole difference. Here is how to pick the right setup.

## An adult birthday (25-50 guests)

The classic milestone birthday in a flat, a shared venue or a garden. It usually runs the same way: guests arrive, people talk, people eat, someone gives a speech, and then there is dancing.

**What do you need?**

- **Under 30 guests indoors:** the small speaker package is perfect. Bluetooth from your phone, and the sound fills the room.
- **30-50 guests, or outdoors:** the large speaker package with two 12" tops. Especially if there are speeches — add a wireless microphone and it plugs straight into the speaker.

**A tip for the speeches:** pause the phone, hand the birthday guest of honour the microphone, and let everyone hear every last embarrassing anecdote. A microphone makes speeches so much better.

## A teenager's party

Teenagers have different expectations from the adults. They want **loud music, bass and party lights**. The large speaker package is the right choice here, together with our light package.

**Tips:**

- Let them make the playlist in advance
- The large package has the power to make it feel like a real party
- Combine it with LED lights and a fog machine — teenagers love it
- Remember to bring the volume down during dinner and the speeches

## A children's birthday

For a children's party you do not need a big system. But a bit of music works wonders for games like musical chairs and dancing competitions.

**What works:**

- The small speaker package is plenty for a children's birthday
- A Bluetooth connection, so you can run the music from your phone
- Put on a "kids' disco" playlist — it is always a hit
- Go easy on the bass — small ears are sensitive

## Indoors versus outdoors

Is the birthday going to be in the garden? Then read our [guide to sound for a garden party](/en/blog/garden-party-sound) for specific tips on outdoor speaker placement.

The short version:

- **Indoors:** the speaker can stand lower, and the walls help reflect the sound
- **Outdoors:** the speaker has to go on a stand, and you need more power (the large package)

## Music tips for a birthday

1. **Make the playlist in advance.** Avoid the awkward "who is in charge of the music" moment.
2. **Start gently.** Lounge music while people arrive and eat.
3. **Build it up.** Change gear after dessert — now it is a party.
4. **Keep a crowd-pleaser list ready.** Songs everyone knows and can sing along to.
