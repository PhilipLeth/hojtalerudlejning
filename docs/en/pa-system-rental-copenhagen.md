# PA system rental in Copenhagen — what you need to know

If you are planning a party, a company event or a garden party in Copenhagen, you may have come across the term "PA system". But what is it, and when do you actually need one? Here are the answers.

## What is a PA system?

PA stands for "public address" — a sound system designed to project audio to an audience. Unlike a Bluetooth speaker, a PA system is built to deliver clear, powerful sound in large rooms and outdoors.

A typical PA system consists of:

- **Tops** (speakers on stands) handling mid-range and treble
- **A subwoofer** taking care of the low end
- **A mixer**, if several sources have to run at once, letting you set levels and connect a microphone, a laptop or a phone
- **Cables and stands**

## When do you need one?

A Bluetooth speaker can handle background music for 10-15 people in a living room. As soon as you go beyond that, you start running short of output:

- **A party with 30+ guests** — the music has to carry over conversation
- **Outdoor events** — sound disperses quickly in the open
- **Events with speeches** — you need a microphone
- **A DJ setup** — a DJ needs real output and monitoring
